# Thumbnail concepts (high CTR)

Size: 1920x1080 (16:9). Keep the subject readable at 300px wide (phone grid size).

## Why the current ones sit at ~2.5%
- Cute purple/gold monster + egg + Roblox noob = looks like many other pet/egg games. Nothing is *ours*.
- The game's real joke (tiny base, ridiculously huge animal) is not shown at all.
- Busy background (volcano, palms, ice, clouds) competes with the subject. Colours all sit in the same blue/green/yellow range as most Roblox thumbnails.
- Title text takes a quarter of the image. The game name already shows under the thumbnail, so use the space for a hook instead.

## Rules for every version
1. One subject, one idea, readable in 1 second.
2. A big face with a strong emotion (open mouth, wide eyes) taking 40%+ of the frame.
3. Scale joke: tiny noob vs giant monster. This is the hook nobody else has.
4. High contrast: warm subject on a cool, simple, blurred background (or the reverse). No more than 3-4 main colours.
5. At most 2-3 words of text, huge, with a thick outline. Or no text.
6. Only show things that exist in the game (honest thumbnail = better retention, no mass-reports).

## Concept A: "Too big for the base" (the joke) <- try first
A tiny Roblox character stands on a small yellow plot with a fence. A gigantic monster towers over, its body
spilling over the fence, one foot crushing the neighbour's plot. Noob looks up with wide eyes, egg in hand.
Text: "TOO BIG?!" or none.

## Concept B: "x500 LUCK" (the payoff)
Close-up of the egg splitting in two with a blinding gold burst, a huge monster face with red eyes behind it.
Big number text "x500" in gold with outline. Dark purple background so the gold pops.

## Concept C: "The throw" (the action)
Low angle from behind the noob mid-throw, egg flying down the long corridor toward a distant tower-sized monster
silhouette on the horizon. Strong speed lines. Text: "THROW IT!"

## Concept D: "Rarest monster" (the chase)
Dark, moody background, a scary monster from a late map (Weeping Dark, Gloomfin, Prism Egg monsters) with
glowing red eyes, a rainbow Prism Egg in the noob's hand. Text: "1 IN 20,000".
(Check the odds shown match the game before using this number.)

## Concept E: "Before / after"
Split frame. Left: tiny cute hatchling. Right: same monster enormous and angry. Arrow between. Text: "GROW".

## Prompt template for an image generator (English)
"Roblox game thumbnail, 16:9, blocky voxel style. [CONCEPT DESCRIPTION]. One clear focal point, big expressive
face, strong rim light, simple blurred background, bold saturated colours with high contrast, thick glossy
cartoon look, no small details, no watermark, no text." (Add the text yourself afterwards in Figma so it is
sharp and spelled right.)

## Testing
Creator Hub > your experience > Thumbnails supports thumbnail A/B tests. Upload 3 (A, B, C) and let it run for
about a week. Judge by CTR; keep the winner, make 3 new variations of it.
